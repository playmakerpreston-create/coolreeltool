Questions

Good to know

What's a seat?

One person, signed in with their own email, on one device at a time. If someone signs in on a second device, the first one is signed out.

How many pages can I manage?

As many as you like. Profiles are unlimited on every plan.

How do I add teammates?

After you subscribe, open Account in the app, add seats, and enter your teammates' emails. They get a sign-in link by email.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Editing and exporting happen in your browser. Your videos never leave your device.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from Billing in the app. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android or desktop Chrome, choose Install app.
